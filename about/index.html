About Barakah Printables

Barakah Printables makes printable activity pages, trackers and planners that help Muslim children and families build good habits: prayer, Quran, dua, good manners and kindness.

Every design follows the same rules: no drawings of faces or people, no music, and nothing that conflicts with Islamic values. Duas and hadith come with their source. If you find a mistake, please tell us and we will fix it for everyone.

Licence

You may print the files as many times as you like for your own family, your own classroom, or your own masjid weekend school. Please do not resell or re-share the PDF files themselves; share a link to this site instead.

Printing

Files are A4. On US Letter paper choose “Fit to page” in the print dialog.
